About

I work on machine learning, deep learning, and biomedical signal processing for healthcare. I am advised by Prof. Reza Sameni at Emory University.

My current research is multimodal physiological modeling for objective mental health assessment. It draws on EEG, ECG, PPG, respiration, electrodermal activity, pupillometry, and psycholinguistic stimuli.

Before this I modeled neural activity and simultaneous EEG-fMRI signals, built deep learning methods for ECG reconstruction and large-scale ECG analysis, applied graph signal processing to multichannel physiological data, and benchmarked and fine-tuned large language models for ECG-related medical question answering.

I was also a member of the organizing team for the George B. Moody PhysioNet Challenges, where I worked on large-scale biomedical data curation, validation, and challenge development. My broader goal is to develop reliable, interpretable, and clinically meaningful AI methods for physiological signal analysis.

Research projects

Oct 2024 to present

A neural network architecture for ECG lead decomposition

Graduate Research Assistant, Emory University. Supervised by Prof. Reza Sameni.

  • Implemented wavelet- and frequency-based approaches as baselines for reconstruction pipelines.
  • Developed a sampling-frequency-agnostic hybrid neural network for ECG lead decomposition that separates shared from lead-specific components.
  • Tested the architecture's robustness by injecting noise and assessing generalization in diagnostic settings.
  • Combined the decomposition network with a residual CNN and attention classifier to measure the diagnostic value of lead-specific components on the PhysioNet/CinC 2021 Challenge data, more than 88,000 12-lead ECG recordings.

Apr 2025 to present

Evaluation and fine-tuning of LLMs for ECG question answering

Graduate Research Assistant, Emory University. Supervised by Prof. Reza Sameni and Prof. Matthew A. Reyna.

  • Built a domain-specific ECG question-answering dataset from medical textbooks, clinical manuals, and conference proceedings using automated LLM-based generation.
  • Ran zero-shot benchmarks of large language models of different sizes, including Gemini, ChatGPT, Mistral, and Gemma, across categories of medical and engineering ECG questions.
  • Identified domain-dependent performance gaps between general-purpose and specialized models.
  • Fine-tuned lightweight language models to reach competitive performance with fewer parameters and better efficiency.

Apr 2021 to May 2023

Generation of synthetic simultaneous EEG-fMRI data

Bachelor's final project, Sharif University of Technology. Supervised by Prof. Arash Amini.

Special B.Sc. Thesis Award Chosen among 200 senior projects in 2021/2022.

  • Modeled spike-generation blocks using stochastic processes and random variables.
  • Designed blocks that generate spatially correlated neural activity.
  • Computed the lead-field matrix by solving the forward problem and clustering the head model.
  • Proposed pipelines that compute fMRI and EEG data consistent with the simulated neural and hemodynamic activity.

Projects

Code from my research and coursework, all on GitHub.

Physiological signals

ECG lead reconstruction with Kalman FIR filtering

An adaptive Kalman filter FIR framework that reconstructs missing ECG leads from a reduced set of leads, evaluated on PTB-XL.

EEG artifact reduction

Artifact removal with blind and semi-blind source separation methods: PCA, GEVD, ICA, and DSS.

Brain source localization

MNE, WMNE, LORETA, and sLORETA source localization applied to epileptic signals.

EEG classification with common spatial patterns

The CSP algorithm with an SVM, separating imagined foot movement from mental subtraction.

EEG classification with genetic algorithms

Feature selection by genetic algorithm, with MLP and RBF networks trained on the selected EEG features.

Dynamics of computational neuron models

Simulations of Hodgkin-Huxley, Izhikevich, and integrate-and-fire neurons and small neural populations, with phase-plane analysis.

Imaging and vision

Medical image denoising and segmentation

Denoising with total variation, anisotropic diffusion, bilateral filtering, non-local means, and wavelets. Segmentation with K-means, FCM, BCFCM, GMM, and deformable models.

Context-aware image captioning

Encoder-decoder captioning on Flickr8k, comparing Inception-V3, EfficientNet-B3, ResNet, U-Net, and Vision Transformer encoders.

Joint object detection and depth estimation

Two concatenated deep neural networks that detect objects and estimate depth together.

Human pose estimation

A CNN that locates 14 body joints in an image, evaluated with the PCP and PDJ metrics.

Language and audio

Tweet sentiment analysis

Sentiment models built with one-directional, bidirectional, and pyramid LSTMs, and with BERT.

Signal denoising with recurrent networks

RNN and LSTM models that denoise audio signals and images.
